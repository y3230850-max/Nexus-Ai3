No coding needed:
1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (name it nexus-ai).
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in EVERYTHING from this unzipped folder
   (including the hidden .github folder), and commit.
3. Open the Actions tab, pick "Build NEXUS AI", click Run workflow.
4. After ~3 minutes open the finished run and download "NEXUS-AI-Windows" at the bottom.
   Inside: "NEXUS AI Setup 1.0.0.exe" (installer) and a portable .exe.
Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app isn't code-signed: click More info > Run anyway.
